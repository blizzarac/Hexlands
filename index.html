Hexlands

A turn-based hex strategy game inspired by Antiyoy

How to play
  • Click one of your provinces (your colour is blue) to select it.
  • Terrain matters: plains earn 1 coin per round, lush meadows earn 2, rocky hills earn nothing — but a tower built on hills defends one level higher. Farms can't be built on hills.
  • Farms add 4 income per level — 6 on meadows, so a maxed meadow farm earns 20 a round. Units cost upkeep every round — if a province goes broke, all its units die.
  • Click a unit, then a highlighted tile to move it. Units move up to their level in tiles per turn (peasant 1 … baron 4), through friendly land only; capturing costs the final step. Freshly bought units can be dropped anywhere in the province.
  • Units capture tiles defended at a strictly lower level than their own — equal defence always blocks, even for level 4.
  • Units, towers and capitals defend their tile and all neighbouring tiles.
  • Merge two units (move one onto the other) to make a stronger one.
  • Towers boost friendly units standing next to them by +1 level (gold ring and +1 badge). The boost applies where the unit stands — park a unit beside a tower and it attacks and defends one level stronger. A boosted level 4 is the only thing that can break a level-4 wall.
  • Chopping a tree earns 3 coins. Trees spread and block income, so keep them in check.
  • Upgrades: with the Tower or Farm button armed, click the existing building (gold outline). A tower becomes a fort (⬡20): defends at level 3 and projects its +1 boost two tiles out. Farms go to level 3 (+4 income each, +6 on meadows).
  • Each AI has a playstyle, shown next to its name: ⚔️ Warlord (attacks relentlessly), 🌾 Builder (farms and expands), 🛡️ Turtle (fortifies), ⚖️ Balanced.
  • Win by destroying every enemy province.
